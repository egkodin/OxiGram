## Telegram Desktop

rgram reproduces the look and behavior of Telegram Desktop
(<https://github.com/telegramdesktop/tdesktop>), Copyright (c) 2014-2026 The
Telegram Desktop Authors, licensed under GPLv3 with an OpenSSL linking
exception. The following material is derived from it:

- `crates/style/src/palette.rs`, generated by `tools/style-gen` from
  `Telegram/lib_ui/ui/colors.palette`;
- the window size constants in `crates/app/src/main.rs`, from
  `Telegram/SourceFiles/window/window.style`.

Each derived file names its source path. Style constants, themes, icons and
fonts are added to this list as later milestones bring them in.
