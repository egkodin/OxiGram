## Telegram Desktop

rgram reproduces the look and behavior of Telegram Desktop
(<https://github.com/telegramdesktop/tdesktop>), Copyright (c) 2014-2026 The
Telegram Desktop Authors, licensed under GPLv3 with an OpenSSL linking
exception. The following material is derived from it:

- `crates/style/src/palette.rs`, generated by `tools/style-gen` from
  `Telegram/lib_ui/ui/colors.palette`;
- the other files in `crates/style/src/` (`basic.rs`, `widgets.rs`,
  `intro.rs`, `window.rs` and the rest), generated by `tools/style-gen` from
  the `.style` files each one names in its first line;
- `crates/style/icons/`, icon masks copied from `Telegram/Resources/icons` and
  `Telegram/lib_ui/icons`;
- `crates/i18n/src/english.rs`, generated by `tools/lang-gen` from
  `Telegram/Resources/langs/lang.strings` (rgram's own strings come from
  `crates/i18n/rgram.strings`);
- the sign-in screens in `crates/features/src/intro/`, the box layer and the
  widgets in `crates/ui/`, which port the logic and layout of the tdesktop
  files named in their module comments;
- the window size constants in `crates/app/src/main.rs`, from
  `Telegram/SourceFiles/window/window.style`.

Each derived file names its source path.
